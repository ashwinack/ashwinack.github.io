      About

      I build the software that makes a wall, a floor or a room respond to the people in it.

      Since 2024 I have worked at Lazulite in the UAE, where I develop hardware-integrated applications, LiDAR touch walls, projection mapping and laser shows for events and exhibitions.

      A project usually starts at the hardware: a sensor, a serial port or a network message, turned into something a visitor can see and play with in real time.

      I trained in Unity game development at SelfStack, then spent over a year as a game developer intern. I’m from Kozhikode in Kerala, and I speak English, Malayalam and Hindi.
