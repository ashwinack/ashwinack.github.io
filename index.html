About

I’m a creative game and application developer who works in Unity and C#. I like projects where software has to meet something physical: sensors, projectors, lasers and custom controllers.

At Lazulite in the UAE I build interactive applications, hardware-integrated installations, LiDAR touch walls and laser shows for live events and exhibitions.

Before that I trained in Unity game development at SelfStack, then spent over a year as a game developer intern.

I enjoy volleyball, football, music and gaming. I speak English, Malayalam and Hindi.

Tools Unity, Unreal Engine, TouchDesigner, Resolume, MadMapper, WatchOut and Pangolin Beyond.
